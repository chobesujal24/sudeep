Modern commercial LED post top lights are becoming more and more common, and like LED street lights, they can illuminate outdoor spaces at night. They far surpass traditional lighting fixtures in terms of luminous efficacy, lifespan, aesthetic design, and overall user experience. They offer a dual solution for environment beautification and enhancing lighting experience.

  • High protection levels IP66, IK08 and surge protection, strong weather resistance, and can work stably in harsh outdoor environments such as rain, snow, sandstorms, and humid heat.
  • Integrated LED structure, it eliminates the need for frequent bulb or ballast replacements like traditional lighting fixtures, enabling maintenance-free operation for over ten years.
  • Supports intelligent control, enabling dimming, automatic sensing control from dusk to dawn, or remote management, further improving energy efficiency and ease of use.
  • Maximum power of 100W, it can even directly replace LED street lights on some smaller roads.

Proper height and spacing when installing LED post top lights can significantly improve lighting uniformity, avoid common problems such as glare and dark areas, and allow the lights to achieve optimal energy efficiency.

  • 3-meter installation: 8–12 meter spacing, low-height lighting, therefore the spacing cannot be too large.
  • 3–4-meter installation: 12–16 meter spacing, more reasonable coverage and better uniformity, the most commonly used configuration for commercial projects.
  • 4–5-meter installation: 15–20 meter spacing, increased height allows for appropriately larger spacing, but continuous lighting must still be maintained.
  • 5–6-meter: 18–22 meter spacing, similar to low-power streetlights, but still classified as pole-top light type.

How to choose the color temperature of outdoor LED post lights?

In actual lighting projects, different color temperatures affect the atmosphere of environment, visual clarity, and security sense in space. Especially in complex outdoor and commercial environments, incorrect color temperature for LED post-top lights may make an originally beautiful landscape appear cold; while choosing the right color temperature can make the entire area warm, friendly, and safer at night. Choose low color temperature for leisure and relaxation areas and high for areas requiring high visibility.

  • 1800K-2200K amber light has very low blue light content, making it more friendly to humans and animals.
  • 3000K light has yellowish tint, creating warm and comfortable feel. It’s suitable for residential areas, pedestrian walkways, park landscapes  and gardens. Warm light is more eye-friendly, with minimal glare, and won’t disturb residents or pedestrians.
  • 4000K strikes balance between white and yellow, currently the most widely used color temperature. It’s natural and not too glaring, making it suitable for areas like commercial street, squares and urban landscape roads.
  • 5000K-6000K provides more focused and brighter visual experience, enhances sense of security. Suitable for parking lots, urban main roads and key monitoring areas.

What are commercial LED post top lights?

Commercial grade LED post top lights are professional lighting fixtures installed on outdoor poles tops  in roads, parks, residential areas, or commercial areas. Using high-efficiency LED chips as their core, they provide uniform, safe, and energy-efficient ambient lighting for outdoor spaces through optimized lens optics and weather-resistant aluminum structures. Different from home or decorative types, they emphasize functionality, lighting stability, and low maintenance, making them commonly considered solution in modern municipal projects.

What should be the installation height for outdoor LED post lights?

Typical installation height is generally 2–6 meters, which can be adjusted according to illumination requirements and coverage area. Lower installations result in brighter pavement and clearer visual details. If you want to be brighter, lower height would be more suitable, such as 2-3 meters. However, outdoor LED post lights installed at heights of 4-6 meters offer wider illumination coverage, typically softer light, and can reduce the number of fixtures required, thus lowering the overall cost.

Do LED post top lights fit the dark sky concept for outdoor use?

Yes, the optical design and color temperature options of LED post top lights fully meet the requirements of the Dark Sky concept. We employ full-cutoff optical design to ensure that the light is projected strictly downwards, eliminating sky glare and preventing disturbance to plants, animals, or nearby residents. Simultaneously, precision lenses further limit lateral light spill and reduce light pollution, ensuring that the light falls only on the area that needs illumination.

Furthermore, color temperature of 3000K or lower is available, ensuring safety and visibility while minimizing the attraction of flying insects and disturbance to nocturnal animals, making it particularly suitable for environments with high animal activity.

What is LED dusk to dawn post top light?

This is a type of outdoor post top lights with photocell sensor, which can automatically turn fixtures on and off according to the ambient brightness. Its principle is to monitor light changes in real time through top- or side-mounted light sensor. It automatically turns on when below a set value, and automatically turns off otherwise, thus providing illumination from dusk to dawn.

  • More energy efficient: Prevents accidental daytime activation, reducing energy waste;
  • Safer: Avoids safety issues caused by forgetting to turn on the lights;
  • Smarter: No additional timer or manual management required;
  • More weather resistant: The sensor structure is suitable for long-term outdoor.

What are the recommended wattage or installation height for LED garden post lights?

In environments such as gardens or courtyards, the emphasis is placed on atmosphere and visual comfort. When installing LED post top lights, it is recommended to choose combination of low power and low installation height, such as 10W-30W and height of 1-3 meters. This creates softer, non-glaring light, avoiding disruption to the plant landscape and nighttime atmosphere.

What is complete solution for LED post lights in parking lots?

The recommended illuminance for parking lot lighting is 15-20 lux, which ensures safety while avoiding severe glare. Therefore, if LED post lights are used in parking lots, power of 60-100W is recommended, with installation height of 4-6 meters, to provide wider coverage while maintaining adequate illuminance uniformity.

For layout, use symmetrical or staggered light poles, maintaining spacing of approximately 12–18 meters to ensure sufficient visibility for both vehicles and pedestrian walkways. 4000-5000K is recommended for clearer vision and better concentration.
